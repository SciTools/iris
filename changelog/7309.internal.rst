:user:`claude`, :user:`trexfeathers`, :user:`stephenworsley`, :user:`pp-mo`,
:user:`bjlittle`, and OpenAI's Codex
split the CF loading and saving code out of
:mod:`iris.fileformats.netcdf` into :mod:`iris.fileformats.cf`, so that it is
shared by the netCDF and Zarr backends rather than owned by one of them. Both
backends reach their storage through one abstraction,
:class:`iris.fileformats.cf.dataset.CFDataset`. The public netCDF names are
unchanged.
